Signal rules

BUY: EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21 and RSI 14 is above 50. SELL: EMA 9 crosses below EMA 21 and RSI 14 is below 50. Stop distance = 2 × ATR(14); take-profit distance = 2 × stop distance. 1% is a risk-sizing target, not a guaranteed loss cap. Check spread, slippage and position size in Exness before entering.

Signals are generated only when a completed candle confirms the crossover. WAIT means no new confirmed setup. This is a technical rule set, not a prediction or financial advice.
